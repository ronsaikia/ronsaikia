
# Analog & Mixed-Signal Electronics

### Electrical Engineering Undergraduate | Aspiring Analog IC Design Engineer

I'm an Electrical Engineering undergraduate with a strong interest in **Analog and Mixed-Signal Integrated Circuit Design**, particularly transistor-level CMOS circuits, amplifier design, device-level nonidealities, and circuit simulation.

I enjoy understanding how circuits behave from first principles, translating analytical predictions into SPICE simulations, and investigating the differences between ideal theory and practical circuit behaviour.

## Technical Focus

- **Analog Electronics:** MOSFETs, BJTs, biasing, current mirrors, differential amplifiers, small-signal analysis
- **Circuit Characterization:** DC operating points, AC frequency response, transient analysis, distortion, CMRR
- **Tools:** LTspice, KiCad
- **Hardware:** Schematic design, PCB layout, embedded electronics and measurement systems
